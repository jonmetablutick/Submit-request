Welcome

Welcome to Social Support Hub. Here you can find general information about account security, privacy settings and staying safe online.

Account Security Tips

  • Use a strong and unique password.
  • Enable two-factor authentication where available.
  • Never share your login information.
  • Beware of suspicious links and messages.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I secure my account?

Use a strong password and enable additional security features offered by the platform.


What should I do if I notice suspicious activity?

Change your password immediately and contact the official support service for the platform you use.
